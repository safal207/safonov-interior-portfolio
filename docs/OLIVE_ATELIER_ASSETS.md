The updated AI concept is informed by the original room image and the selected sofa, armchair, coffee table and cabinet photographs, with the lamp and textile details described from their original product images. It communicates colour relationships and furniture forms. Exact product geometry, placement, clearances and exterior views require the same measured design work as the original case.
