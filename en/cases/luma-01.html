Process

From an idea
to an inspectable scene.

  1. 01 / Geometry

    Rounded cushions, timber armrests, supports, rails and splayed legs. Dimensions are specified in metres.

  2. 02 / Materials and light

    Procedural timber and fabric shading, soft shadows and ambient occlusion in a software CPU renderer.

  3. 03 / Camera and export

    Four stills and 144 animation frames. Geometry is also exported as GLB and OBJ from the same scene.json.

Open source files

Look closer.

GLB and OBJ contain geometry and basic material colours. Procedural render textures are reproduced by the code and are not baked into the model.
